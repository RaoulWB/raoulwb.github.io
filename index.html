About Me

Raoul William Braghieri

That’s me! I was born in 2001 in Milan and spent half of my life in Rome.

When I was a kid I was convinced I would eventually become an engineer, which led me to getting a scientific education in my teen years. However, as the school years passed, my passions grew ever-more varied, so by the time I had to pick a University course I decided to study Media Arts at the Fine Arts Academy of Rome, from which I graduated with honours in 2024.
